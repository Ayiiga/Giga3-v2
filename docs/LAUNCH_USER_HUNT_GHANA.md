## Audiences (Ghana, adults 18–44)

1. **AI / technology** — curious professionals and students who want practical AI apps.
2. **Education / teachers / e-learning / STEM** — BECE/WASSCE support, lesson help, GigaLearn.
3. **Creators / business / content** — posters, short video, GigaEdit starter pack, client work.

## Creative concepts (organic first)

### 1) “10 Apps”
- Promise: One Giga3 account covers the AI jobs people juggle across many apps.
- CTA: “Open Chat free” → first useful answer in under 2 minutes.
- Channels: TikTok/Reels listicle; YouTube Shorts; Facebook carousel.

### 2) “Learn Smarter”
- Promise: Ghana-context learning help (GigaLearn + academic personas).
- CTA: “Try a free tutor question today” (15/day free advanced persona allowance when shipped).
- Channels: Facebook groups for teachers/students; YouTube explainers; WhatsApp status (organic).

### 3) “Create & Grow”
- Promise: Images, short video, and GigaEdit starter templates for local businesses.
- CTA: “Make one flyer in Media Studio”.
- Channels: TikTok before/after; YouTube demo; Facebook creator pages.

## Demo video + organic content plan

- **Demo (60–90s):** Homepage → sign-up → chat answer → Media Studio still → GigaLearn card → install PWA tip.
- **Weekly organic cadence (proposal):** 3 short videos (one per concept) + 2 static posts + 1 founder note.
- **Platforms:** Facebook, TikTok, YouTube. No boosts until approval.

## Referral / community (proposal)

- Soft referral: “Invite a classmate/colleague” without automatic rewards until fraud controls reviewed.
- Community: seed a Ghana early-user WhatsApp/Telegram with feedback form only (opt-in).

## Paid media (proposal only)
