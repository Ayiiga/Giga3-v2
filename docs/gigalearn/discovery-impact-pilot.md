# GigaLearn Discovery World pilot protocol

**Status:** Ready for educator review and school approval. No learners have been recruited or measured under this protocol.

The 90-card Discovery release and its automated tests establish that the pictures, spoken prompts and game are available. They do not establish vocabulary gains, retention or classroom effectiveness. This pilot produces the first local outcome evidence that can accompany a funding application.

## Evaluate one learning outcome

Can KG learners independently name familiar objects after learning with GigaLearn, and can they use those names with different pictures or real objects? Use one group of 12 teacher-reviewed fruit and household words and four unfamiliar examples for transfer. Ask Ghanaian KG teachers to review the words, the accepted Ghanaian English and local-language equivalents, the images, pronunciation, and age appropriateness before recruitment. Record the assessment language and accept the same approved variants at every time point. Keep the item list, images, instructions and scoring key versioned.

| When | Task | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Week 0 | Show one image at a time and ask “What is this?” without a hint | Baseline independent naming out of 12 |
| Weeks 1–6 | Run two short, supervised Discovery activities each week, with real objects where possible | Sessions offered and attended, assistance level, and interruptions |
| Week 7 | Repeat naming using equivalent photos in a different order. Then show four real objects or unfamiliar photos | Post-test naming out of 12 and transfer out of 4 |
| Weeks 8 and 11 | Repeat the naming task with rotated equivalents and no reminder | Seven- and 30-day recall out of 12, with actual days recorded |

For each item mark **1** for an independently named object and **0** for a wrong answer, “I don't know” or a teacher hint. Mark an item **missing** when it could not be administered. Give the same neutral encouragement in both groups and avoid giving answers before the assessment is finished. Use a second scorer for a sample of observations when available and report agreement.

## Design and analysis

- For a feasibility pilot, report the paired change from baseline to week 7, the observed range or confidence interval, and denominators at every follow-up. Show how many learners were eligible, consented, started, completed and were lost to follow-up. Explain missingness and do not silently count missing tasks as wrong.
- If at least two suitable classes are available, use a planned comparison with similar teaching time and ordinary instruction. Where practical, allocate at classroom level and use assessors who do not know each class's assignment. A small nonrandom comparison can inform the next study. It cannot isolate GigaLearn's effect from class or teacher differences.
- Before the first assessment, agree the sample, comparison, primary endpoint, success thresholds and analysis with participating educators. Keep implementation measures separate from outcomes, and report device and network difficulties even if they lower completion.
- Share only anonymised aggregates externally. Obtain school approval, caregiver consent and age-appropriate assent before collecting learner data. Use random learner codes. Keep any code-to-name key separate with restricted school access, and agree the deletion schedule before collection. Do not collect photos or voice recordings for this pilot.

## Field record

Use one private row per learner and assessment visit. Record `learner_code, class_code, group, grade, assessment_language, instrument_version, visit, days_since_posttest, items_administered, correct_independent, transfer_items_administered, transfer_correct, sessions_offered, sessions_attended, missing_reason`. Record device/network barriers and teacher comments separately in aggregate. Keep functionality evidence such as release links and uptime in a separate appendix.

A funding summary should state the sample, design, language, assessment version, paired change, retention and transfer figures, and limitations. Until the pilot runs, use “GigaLearn is designed to support vocabulary practice and will measure independent naming, retention and transfer in a supervised pilot.”
