About Giga3 AI

Giga3 AI is an AI platform focused on helping businesses and developers use smarter automation—ranging from enterprise voice/customer support agents to codebase context tools that improve AI coding accuracy. Its core strength is giving AI better context so it can solve complex tasks faster, with fewer mistakes.

Founder and location

Designed and founded by Ayiiga Benard Issaka (Young Anointed), a basic school educationist from Ghana — Intelligence Global Arena (GIGA).

Mission

Bring AI chat, research, coding help, learning, image and video creation, a creator community and a marketplace priced in Ghanaian cedis into one mobile-ready platform for students, creators and businesses in Ghana, Africa and the world.

Technology

  • Next.js progressive web app deployed on Cloudflare Pages
  • Convex realtime backend (functions, scheduling, file storage)
  • Multi-provider AI failover on the server: OpenAI → Gemini → OpenRouter (via fal.ai)
  • fal.ai and Replicate for image and video generation
  • Paystack billing in GHS — cards, mobile money and bank
