GigaLearn classroom sample — JHS 1 English
Subject–verb agreement • 40 minutes
Original example by the Giga3 AI editorial team • 19 November 2026

Objective
Learners correctly choose the verb in four out of five present-tense sentences and explain one choice.

Materials
Chalkboard, chalk, sentence cards and exercise books.
Curriculum indicator
Teacher to select the current NaCCA indicator and match the school scheme.

Starter (5 minutes)
Write “Ama walks to school” and “The pupils walk to school”. Ask learners to identify the subject and verb in each sentence.

Modelling (10 minutes)
Explain that a singular third-person subject usually takes an -s or -es verb in the simple present. Plural subjects take the base verb. I and you also take the base verb: I walk; you walk.

Paired task (15 minutes)
Choose and explain each answer.
1. The boy (read / reads) every day.
2. The children (play / plays) at break time.
3. My sister (wash / washes) her hands.
4. I (write / writes) neatly.
5. You (help / helps) your friends.
Answers: 1 reads; 2 play; 3 washes; 4 write; 5 help.

Exit ticket and feedback (10 minutes)
1. Kojo ___ a book. (reads)
2. The girls ___ football. (play)
3. My friend ___ early. (arrives)
Identify learners who need further examples and revisit the subject before the verb.
