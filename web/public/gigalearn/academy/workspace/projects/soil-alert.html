Soil Alert Simulator

This uses a fictional calibrated moisture scale: 0 means dry and 100 means wet. Real sensors require their own calibration. No physical device is connected.

The alert turns on below 30, turns off above 40, and keeps its previous state between those thresholds. Missing input produces a fault with the simulated output off.
